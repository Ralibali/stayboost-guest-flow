# StayBoost — Go-to-market

## Positioning

Do not sell StayBoost as “another hotel system”.

Sell the operational outcome:

> Färre manuella bokningsmoment. Färre missar. Mer direktbokning och mer intäkt per gäst.

Alternative short promise:

> Bokningar, gäster och merförsäljning — utan att små boenden behöver bygga en reception.

## Initial ICP

Prioritize Sweden first and businesses where the owner/operator still does much of the work personally:

- glamping
- cabins / cottage villages
- B&Bs
- small campsites
- small boutique accommodation
- farm stays
- other 1–20 unit independent properties

Good prospect signals:

- active on Booking.com/Airbnb
- own website exists
- weak or absent direct-booking flow
- manual guest SMS/email
- breakfast, bike, spa, late checkout or similar ancillary sales potential
- owner-operated / small team
- visible friction between website, OTA calendar and guest operations

Avoid trying to win large hotels first.

## Wedge

The initial wedge is not “replace every PMS”.

Offer:

1. Connect current booking sources.
2. Get one operational calendar/Today view.
3. Automate guest communication.
4. Add a high-converting direct-booking path.
5. Sell add-ons before and during the stay.
6. Surface sync/payment/guest problems before they become emergencies.

This lets StayBoost coexist with an existing channel manager and later earn the right to replace more of the stack.

## Pilot offer

Keep the first sale extremely easy.

Suggested founding offer to validate willingness to pay:

- 14-day assisted pilot
- setup included for the first limited cohort
- connect property, units and at least one existing calendar source
- configure direct booking + guest messages
- configure 1–3 real add-ons
- review after first real bookings/stays

Pricing should be tested rather than treated as permanent. A reasonable first hypothesis is a single simple plan around the price point small hosts already accept for channel/PMS software, with SMS/payment provider usage passed through transparently.

Do not permanently underprice before retention and support cost are known.

## Standard pricing hypothesis to test

Start simple:

**StayBoost — 499 SEK/month/property**

Includes:

- booking/admin core
- direct-booking engine
- guest page
- automated guest communication workflows
- add-ons/upsells
- operations/Today
- revenue overview
- supported calendar/channel connections

Possible later expansion:

- higher tier for multi-property / advanced channel automation / AI revenue automation
- additional property fee
- optional implementation/migration package

Do not add five pricing tiers before data proves a need.

## Sales motion

### 1. Build a list from real operational pain

Research prospects, not random email addresses.

For each account capture:

- property name
- city/region
- approximate units
- website
- visible booking path
- OTA presence
- obvious direct-booking friction
- likely add-on opportunity
- personalized reason StayBoost may help

### 2. Personalized outbound

Send low-volume, high-relevance outreach.

Example structure:

> Hej [namn], jag tittade på [boende] och såg att ni tar bokningar via [kanal/website observation]. Jag driver själv boendeverksamhet och har byggt StayBoost för att samla kalender/drift, automatisera gästkommunikation och sälja tillval utan mer administration. Jag tror särskilt [specific observation] skulle vara relevant för er. Vill du att jag visar hur det skulle se ut med just era rum/stugor på 15 minuter?

Do not mass-spam generic “AI hospitality” copy.

### 3. Demo using their reality

The best demo is not a feature tour.

Show:

- their units
- one Booking/Airbnb-style imported stay
- today’s arrivals/departures
- guest message automation
- one add-on sale
- direct booking flow
- channel health / conflict protection

Then ask:

> Vilken del av detta gör ni manuellt idag?

### 4. Assisted activation

Success target:

**live within 30 minutes for a simple property** once credentials/calendars are available.

Track time-to-live.

## Product-led proof

A demo account should always contain coherent, realistic sample data and make the value obvious within 60 seconds.

Demo story:

1. New Booking.com reservation arrives.
2. StayBoost calendar/Today updates.
3. Guest receives scheduled message.
4. Guest purchases breakfast/bike/spa/late checkout.
5. Operator sees operational requirement.
6. Revenue view attributes extra revenue.
7. A synchronization problem is surfaced with inventory protected.

## Case-study template

Use verified customer/property data only.

Track before vs after:

- minutes of manual admin/week
- direct-booking share
- add-on revenue/stay
- message automation rate
- missed-message incidents
- calendar-sync interventions
- occupancy/ADR/RevPAR where data quality supports it

Case headline should be an outcome, not “implemented StayBoost”.

## Acquisition channels in order

1. founder-led targeted outbound
2. referrals from existing hospitality operators
3. partnerships with web agencies / tourism consultants / photographers / accountants serving small accommodation
4. niche SEO with strong commercial intent
5. relevant accommodation-owner groups/communities without spam
6. paid search only after conversion and activation are proven

## SEO themes once sales language is proven

Examples of commercial/problem intent:

- bokningssystem för små boenden
- bokningssystem stuguthyrning
- bokningssystem B&B
- bokningssystem glamping
- channel manager små boenden
- Booking.com kalender synk
- automatiska gästmeddelanden hotell/stugor
- sälja frukost/tillval till gäster
- alternativ till [competitor] only where comparisons are factual and useful

Do not mass-generate city pages before genuine search demand and product traction.

## Sales KPIs

Weekly:

- qualified prospects researched
- personalized outreaches
- replies
- demos booked
- pilots started
- activated properties
- paying properties
- MRR
- demo → pilot conversion
- pilot → paid conversion
- time to activation
- 30/90-day retention

## Scale rule

Do not pour paid traffic into a product that does not activate and retain customers.

Scale in this order:

RELIABILITY → ACTIVATION → RETENTION → CASE STUDIES → REPEATABLE SALES → SEO/PAID SCALE

When one segment converts materially better (for example glamping or cabin operators), narrow the ICP and double down before broadening again.
